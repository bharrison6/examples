How to play Zero to Unbeatable

You are playing tic-tac-toe against an AI that begins with no strategy at all.

  1. Play Era 0. It chooses randomly, so you should be able to beat it.
  2. Press Train the AI. It plays thousands of games, keeps what worked, and creates a new era.
  3. Play the new era, then train again. Use Show its brain to see the live value learned for each square.
  4. Continue until the app proves there is no line of play that can beat it.

What the colors mean

Gold squares look promising to the AI; red-orange squares look dangerous. Era 0 has all zeros because it genuinely knows nothing.
