How to play

You have a spacecraft, a fuel tank of Δv, and a mission. Lowest Δv wins.

Flying

Time-warp with the ⏸ / 1× / 10× / 50× / 200× buttons, or jump straight to periapsis (→ Pe, lowest point, fastest) or apoapsis (→ Ap, highest point, slowest). Space pauses. . and , change warp.

Burning

Hit 🔥 Plan burn (or B). Pick a direction — prograde (with your motion), retrograde (against it), radial, or a free angle — and drag the Δv slider. The dotted line previews your new orbit before you spend fuel. COMMIT applies it instantly.

On later levels the engine is finite: committed Δv is delivered over time (the orange arc in the preview shows the powered stretch), so start your burn before periapsis to centre it on the fast point — and with a weak engine, split it into repeated perigee kicks. X or ✂ cuts the engine early, keeping unspent fuel.

The lesson hiding in the game

Adding Δv changes your kinetic energy by ΔKE = v·Δv + ½Δv². The v·Δv term means the same burn buys more energy when you're already moving fast — and you're fastest at periapsis. That's the Oberth effect. Open the Physics HUD and watch "energy per unit Δv" climb as you fall toward periapsis.
