FINALETakeaways
  1. “AI” is bigger than chatbots. The tic-tac-toe learner is real AI with no language in it at all. When something is labeled AI, ask what kind — it is often not generative AI.
  2. Generative AI is not magic. It is a predictor trained on data; reasoning models and agents are layers built on top. You watched every layer get built in Glass Box.
  3. The answer is only as good as the ask. Context, a success spec, and the domain’s own vocabulary are free performance. When you get a bad answer, ask which lever you left unpulled.
  4. The curve is steeper than you drew it. Capability has been compounding for years and the gaps between releases keep shrinking. Plan for the trend, not the snapshot.
  5. The opportunity is in your discipline. Pair AI with your subject expertise and you get tools like the four above — tailored, classroom-ready, and fast to build. The tools are ready for your field.
